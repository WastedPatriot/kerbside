# Authorised use

kerbside is for work you are allowed to be doing: your own lab, a range, HTB, exam practice, or an engagement with a scope and a signature.

It reads files you already have (BloodHound JSON, ldapsearch LDIF, a flat CSV). It does not connect to LDAP, Kerberos, DNS, or anything else with a pulse. It does not request tickets, dump hashes, or run exploits.

Do not point it at a customer export you are not cleared to hold. Do not bolt an exploitation kit onto it and pretend that is still this tool.

If you are staring at a production directory and nobody has said yes, close the laptop.
